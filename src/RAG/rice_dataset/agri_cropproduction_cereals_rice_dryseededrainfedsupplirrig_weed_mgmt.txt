Second  weeding may be done 30 - 45 days after first weeding.

Apply pre emergence (PE) herbicide Pendimethalin 1.0 kg/ha on 5 DAS followed by one Hand weeding on 30 to 35 DAS.

.

Application of Pretilachlor@0.45 kg/ha on 5 DAS and two machine weeding (Power weeder) on 30 and 45 DAS, if sowing is done by using Happy seeder.
