Foliar spray of KCl 1% to overcome moisture stress at different physiological stages of rice.

First top dressing should be done immediately after the receipt of sufficient rain or canal water.

Hand weeding, thinning and gap filling should be done before N-fertilizer application.

Subsequent top dressings in two or three splits should be done before heading.
