Dryseeded Irrigated Un-Puddled Home | Seasons & Varieties | Tillage | Nutrient Mgmnt | Irrigation Mgmnt | Weed Mgmnt | Crop Protection | Cost of Cultivation Photobank Crop Production :: Cereal :: Rice :: Dryseeded Irrigated Un-Puddled Also be called ‘semi-dry rice’ It is a contingent  plan for  command areas,  anticipating the release of water; rice crop has to be established under rainfed  condition up to a maximum of 45 days.  Field is converted to wet condition on  receipt of canal water. Conversion depends up on receipt of canal water and  nutrient management is decided according to the period of irrigation.

Area Tiruvarur  and Nagapattinam districts Season Samba  / Thaladi seasons in command areas.

Varieties Medium duration varieties, if sown in August and short  duration varieties beyond September, Since there is assured irrigation from canal, high yielding  improved short or medium duration varieties can be cultivated depending up on  the situation .
