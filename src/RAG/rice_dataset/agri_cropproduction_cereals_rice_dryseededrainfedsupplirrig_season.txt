Area Kanchipuram/Tiruvallur,  Ramanathapuram, Sivaganga, Kanyakumari, Nagapattinam/ Tiruvarur and Pudukottai.

Seasons July  to August  - Kanchipuram/Tiruvallur,  Kanyakumari August  - Nagapattinam/Tiruvarur,  Pudukottai September  to October - Ramanathapuram, Sivaganga Varieties Short duration varieties  including local land races suitable for those tracts.
