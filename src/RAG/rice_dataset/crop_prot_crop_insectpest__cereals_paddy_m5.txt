TNAU Agritech Portal :: Crop Protection Success Stories Farmers Association Farmers' Innovation Publications Contact Crop Protection  :: Pest of paddy Leaf folder (or) leaf roller:

Cnaphalocrocis medinalis  / Marasmia patnalis Symptom of damage:

Larva folds the leaves  longitudinally and  remains inside Larva scrapes the green tissues      of the leaves, which later  becomes white and dry During severe infestation the      whole field exhibits scorched appearance Identification of insect pest:

Egg:

Flat,      oval in shape and yellowish white in colour Larva:

Greenish      translucent Adult:

Moth      is yellowish brown with many dark wavy lines in centre and dark band on margin of      wings.

Management:

ETL:

10% leaf damage at vegetative phase and 5% of flag leaf damage at flowering Release Trichogramma      chilonis @2 cc (40,000/ac)

thrice at 30, 37 and 44 days after planting Avoid excessive nitrogenous fertilizers Keep the bunds clean Spray NSKE 5 % or Azadirachtin 0.03% 400 ml/ac Spray any one of the following insectcides Chlorpyriphos 20 EC 500 ml/ac Acephate 75 % SP 267-400 ml/ac Carbosulfan 6% G 6.7 kg/ac CartapHydrochloride 50 % SP 400 g/ac Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC 60 g/ac Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% G 4 kg/ac Fipronil 80%WG 20-25 g/ac Flubendiamide 39.35% M/M SC 20 g/ac Flubendiamide 20% WG 50-100 g/ac Thiamethoxam 25% WG 40 g/ac white and dried leaves Egg Larva Adult Trichogramma chilonis egg card Success Stories Farmers Association Farmers' Innovation Publications Disclaimer © 2008-2026 TNAU.
