Use of LCC has more advantage in N management.

.

N management through LCC or Apply fertilizer nutrients as per STCR-IPNS .

Time  of application is decided by LCC score.

Take  observations from 14 DAT in transplanted rice or 21 days after sowing in direct seeded rice.

Repeat  the observations  at weekly intervals up  to heading.

Observe  the leaf colour in the fully opened third leaf from the top as index leaf.

Match  the leaf color with the colours in the chart during morning hours (8-10 am).

Take  observation in 10 places.

LCC  critical value is 3.0 in low N response cultivars  like White Ponni and 4.0 in  other cultivars and hybrids When  6/10 observations show less than the critical colour value, N can be applied @  35kg N/ha in dry season and 30kg N/ha in wet season per application per ha.
