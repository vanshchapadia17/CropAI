DRY SEEDED RAINFED UN-PUDDLED LOWLAND RICE Home | Seasons & Varieties | Tillage | Nutrient Mgmnt | Irrigation Mgmnt | Weed Mgmnt | Crop Protection | Cost of Cultivation Photobank Crop Production :: Cereals :: Rice :: Rainfed Rice Rainfed  Rice Crop establishment, growth and maturity depend up on the rainfall received.

There will be standing water after crop establishment for a minimum period of  few days to a maximum up to grain filling, depending up on the rainfall.

This  type of cultivation in Tamil Nadu is called as ‘rainfed rice’ , with the  assumption that the soil moisture will be under unsaturated (dry) condition  during establishment or entire growth period, with reference to tropical  climate.

Area Coastal  districts of Tamil Nadu like Kanchipuram, Tiruvallur, Pudukottai,  Ramanathapuram, Virudhunagar, Sivagangai and Kanyakumari.

Season June  – July – (Coastal northern districts)

September  – October (Coastal southern districts)

Varieties Short duration varieties   including local land races are suitable .
