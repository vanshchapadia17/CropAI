Dual  cropping of rice-green manure is economic for nutrient budget and efficient for  grain production.

Manually drawn seeder has been used to sow pre-germinated paddy and green manure daincha crop ( Sesbania aculeata ) in alternate rows in puddled soil.

After one month of sowing the daincha crop was trampled by cono weeder.

Seeder sows four paddy rows and four daincha rows in a single pass. Paddy was sown at 60 kg/ha seed rate and green manure crop at 20 kg/ha seed rate.
