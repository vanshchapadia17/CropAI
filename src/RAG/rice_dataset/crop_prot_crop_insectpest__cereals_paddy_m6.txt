TNAU Agritech Portal :: Crop Protection Success Stories Farmers Association Farmers' Innovation Publications Contact Crop Protection  :: Pest of paddy Green horned caterpillar:

Melanitis leda ismene Symptom of damage:

Larva feeds on leaf blades of      rice.

Leaves are defoliated from the      margin or tip irregularly Identification of insect pest:

Egg:

white      eggs singly on the leaves Larva:

lightly      flattened with two red horn processes on the head, two yellow processes      in the anal end Pupa:

chrysalis,      which suspends from the leaf Adult:

dark      brown with large wings having a black yellow eye like spot one on each of      the fore wings Management:

Natural biological control agents often keep the larval population under control. For example, the eggs are parasitized by trichogrammatid wasps. Chalcid wasp and two species of tachinid flies parasitize the larvae and a vespid wasp preys on the larvae.

Spray the crop with chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 500 ml/ac if infestation  is severe.
